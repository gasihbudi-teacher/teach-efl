# Simple Present Tense

## At a glance

Use the Simple Present for routines, repeated actions, and facts.

- *I study English every day.* — Saya belajar bahasa Inggris setiap hari.
- *Water boils at 100°C at sea level.* — Air mendidih pada suhu 100°C di permukaan laut.

## Learning objectives

Students can recognise and make basic affirmative Simple Present sentences about routines and facts.

## Teaching path

| Stage | Student action | Support |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Notice routine and fact meanings | Familiar examples |
| 2 | Choose the verb form | I/you/we/they vs he/she/it |

## Teaching stages

### Stage 1 — Notice the meaning

**Teacher explanation:** Simple Present dipakai untuk kebiasaan, kegiatan berulang, dan fakta umum.

**Model:** *She walks to school every day.* — Dia berjalan kaki ke sekolah setiap hari.

**Student task:** Identify the routine or fact.

1. I drink water after exercise.
2. The sun rises in the east.
3. They play badminton on Fridays.

**Answers:** 1. Routine. 2. General fact. 3. Routine.

**Readiness checkpoint:** Students can explain whether a sentence describes a routine or a general fact.

### Stage 2 — Choose the verb form

**Teacher explanation:** Untuk I/you/we/they, gunakan base verb. Untuk he/she/it, biasanya tambahkan -s atau -es.

**Model:** *I play football.* / *He plays football.*

**Student task:** Complete the sentences.

1. She ______ (study) English.
2. We ______ (live) in Indonesia.
3. My brother ______ (watch) films.

**Answers:** 1. studies. 2. live. 3. watches.

**Readiness checkpoint:** Students choose the correct form for the subject.

## Quick review

- I/you/we/they + base verb.
- He/she/it + verb-s/-es (with spelling changes where needed).
